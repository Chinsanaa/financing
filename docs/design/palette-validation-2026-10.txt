Reading the palette results
---------------------------
- Picker order (adjacent pairs — what stacked bars, the picker grid and the
  ranked rows actually place side by side by default): ALL CHECKS PASS in both
  themes, against both the card surface and the page bg. No WARN.
- All-pairs (--pairs all) FAILS in both themes. This is structural, not a
  tuning miss: 12 colors, each locked to its key's hue family (the DB stores
  the key, so "lime" must stay a yellow-green, "green" a green, etc.), with
  >= 3:1 against the paper and no hues in 270-335 deg, cannot be pairwise
  distinct at dE 15. The constrained OKLCH search (random restarts + hill
  climbing, 30+ runs) maximised the all-pairs minimum as its secondary goal:
    light  worst normal dE  3.7 (v4) -> 7.5 (v5);  worst CVD 0.3 -> 4.2
    dark   worst normal dE  2.9 (v4) -> 7.8 (v5);  worst CVD 1.5 -> 3.6
  The closest v5 pairs are rose/orange (Rose vs Rust) and emerald/teal (light),
  cyan/teal and cyan/sky (dark). Five green-family keys on green paper are
  now spread by lightness and hue: light green L .44 (darkest) / lime L .55 /
  emerald L .60 (most saturated, C .16) / teal H 176 / olive H 101; the
  closest green-family pair is emerald/teal at dE 7.6 (light), emerald/green
  at 8.6 (dark) — v4 had emerald/green at 3.7.
- Justification (why this is acceptable): the dataviz "all pairs" mode is for
  scatter/bubble/maps where color is the ONLY identifier. In this app every
  colored mark (Badge swatch, ranked row, stacked-bar legend, picker swatch)
  prints the category NAME in ink beside it (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §1 rule 5), so
  color is never the only signal. Charts show at most a handful of categories
  at once; the picker shows the label on hover/selection.
